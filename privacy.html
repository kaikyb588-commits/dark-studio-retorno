Privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026.

Dark Studio is a private tool used by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no user sign-up.

What we access

  • TikTok basic profile (open ID and display name), to show which account is connected.
  • Posting permission, to upload videos that the owner approved.
  • Video list and counts (views, likes, comments) of the account's public videos, to measure which stories work.

How we store it

  • Access tokens are encrypted (AES-GCM) and stored only on the studio's own server.
  • We do not sell, share or rent any data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not collect data from viewers or other TikTok users.

Removing access

Disconnect the account in the studio, or remove "Dark Studio" in TikTok under Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services. After disconnecting, the stored token is revoked and no longer used.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/kaikyb588-commits/dark-studio-retorno.
