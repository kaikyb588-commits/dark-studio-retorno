Dark Studio

A private studio for short documentary videos about true stories: mythology, history, business and sports.

The owner writes and reviews every script, produces a vertical video with narration and archival images (public domain or openly licensed, always credited), and publishes it to the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts. Every post is reviewed and approved by the owner before it goes out, and AI-assisted content is labeled as such.

How it uses TikTok

  • Log in with TikTok to connect the owner's own account.
  • Post a finished video to that account, only after the owner chooses who can see it, the interaction settings and the caption, and approves the post.
  • Read the view, like and comment counts of the posted videos to plan the next stories.

Português: o Dark Studio é um estúdio particular de vídeos curtos de documentário. Cada post é revisado e aprovado pelo dono antes de sair.
